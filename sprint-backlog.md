# Sprint Backlog

### Evidence
* **Sprint Goal:** Complete interactive UI screen navigation and update product backlog items.
* **Sprint Items:** Review backlog, design remaining UI screens, improve prototype navigatio.
* **Completed:** Trello Product Backlog updated, UI completed, Sprint review completed.

### Reflection
During this sprint, our team initially over-committed by pulling in too many complex database integration stories alongside our user interface design tasks. Through daily stand-ups, we recognized that our team velocity could not support both heavy backend development and front-end prototyping simultaneously without compromising quality.Recognizing our capacity constraints, we adjusted our focus during our mid-sprint review. We deprioritized complex backend server queries for that specific sprint, reallocating our efforts entirely toward perfecting the core mobile UI navigation and user report intake forms. This flexibility proved why iterative sprint reviews and backlog grooming prevent team burnout.Monitoring our sprint completion rate taught us valuable lessons about realistic workload estimation. Breaking down our sprint backlog into tight, time-bound objectives ensured that every member knew their exact deliverables before moving on to testing and documentation phases.
